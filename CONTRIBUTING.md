# Contributing to TransitMatters Labs Projects

Thanks for your interest! Our projects are built by volunteers with [TransitMatters Labs](https://transitmatters.org/transitmatters-labs), part of the Boston-area nonprofit [TransitMatters](https://transitmatters.org/). If you'd like to volunteer, fill out the [volunteer form](https://transitmatters.org/volunteer).

## Who we work with

TransitMatters is a Boston-area advocacy group, and our projects are about the MBTA and transit in New England. We prioritize contributions from people with a real connection to the region: you live, work, or study here, ride the T, or used to. If that isn't you, we'd encourage you to put your time into a transit or civic tech group closer to home, where local knowledge goes further. PRs from contributors without a connection to the area may be closed without review.

## AI-assisted contributions

You're welcome to use AI coding tools, but a person has to stay in the loop. We don't accept autonomous or "vibe-coded" contributions. Read and understand every change you submit, and be ready to explain it yourself in review. Agents shouldn't open, approve, or merge PRs on their own, and shouldn't run unattended against our repos. Agents should follow the `AGENTS.md` file in the repository they're working in.

## Before you start

- Look through the open issues on the project you're interested in. For anything bigger than a small fix, comment on an issue or open one first so we can agree on the approach.
- Each project's README covers setup, and its `AGENTS.md` lists lint and test commands.

## Pull requests

- Keep each PR to one change, and say why, what changed, and how you tested it.
- If your change touches a UI, include at least one screenshot of it.
- TransitMatters is a volunteer-run nonprofit. If a change would add hosting cost, say so and include an estimate.
- A maintainer reviews every PR before it's merged.
